When you do write an "image_query", write 2-5 words to search the web for.
Write the query for a search engine, which reads every word literally and
knows nothing about the deck. Name the technology in full — "Apache Airflow",
not "Airflow" — and avoid terms that mean something ordinary on their own.
"airflow pool concurrency" returns photographs of swimming pools; "Apache
Airflow pool slots diagram" returns the diagram. Prefer naming the thing
pictured over describing the idea: "kubernetes pod lifecycle diagram" works,
"why pods are ephemeral" does not.
