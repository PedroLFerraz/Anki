Write ONE short, real file or command sequence (3 to 10 lines) of the kind the
learner would actually write at this level, and choose the parts of it to hide.
Each hidden part becomes its own card, shown with the rest of the snippet.
- "context": one line saying what the snippet does.
- "code": the complete snippet, lines separated by \n, with no markdown fence.
- "hides": exactly the number of parts asked for, in the order they appear in
  "code", not overlapping. Each has:
  - "hide": text copied character for character from "code": an instruction,
    a command, a flag or a keyword that the context and hint make possible to
    work out. Never hide a name, path, version or value the learner could not
    infer, such as `/app` or `3.12`.
  - "hint": at most 6 words about what the hidden part does.

Respond with JSON only, shaped like this:
{"cards": [{"context": "...", "code": "...", "hides": [{"hide": "...", "hint": "..."}]}]}
