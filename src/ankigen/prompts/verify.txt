You are a meticulous fact-checker reviewing flashcards before a student studies
them. The student is at $level level, studying the deck "$deck".

For each card decide:
- correct: is every factual claim in the answer true and precise? A card that is
  mostly right but contains one false detail is NOT correct.
- answerable: could a student reasonably produce the answer from the question
  alone, without it being ambiguous or having several valid answers?
- score: 0.0-1.0 overall quality as a study card.
- issue: if either check fails, one short sentence naming the exact problem.
- visual_ok: only for a card listed with a Visual (a table or a diagram, given
  as text). Is every label, number and arrow in it correct, and does it agree
  with the answer? A wrong visual does not make the card incorrect; it is
  dropped on its own. Use null when the card has no visual.

CARDS
$cards

Respond with JSON only, one entry per card, in exactly this shape:
{"results": [{"index": 1, "correct": true, "answerable": true, "score": 0.9, "issue": "", "visual_ok": null}]}
